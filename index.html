FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and booking process.

How do I book an appointment? +

You can book online through our appointment form, call us directly, or visit our clinic. Our team will confirm your slot within 2 hours during working hours.

What heart conditions do you treat? +

We treat all major heart conditions including coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, high blood pressure, heart failure, valve disorders, and preventive heart care.

Do you offer emergency consultations? +

Yes, our emergency cardiac care unit is available 24/7 for chest pain, arrhythmias, and other urgent heart conditions.

What insurance plans are accepted? +

We accept most major insurance plans. Please contact our office to confirm if your specific plan is covered.

How long does a first consultation take? +

A first consultation usually takes 30 to 45 minutes, including a full evaluation, discussion of your history, and a personalized care plan.
